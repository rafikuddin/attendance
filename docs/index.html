Attendance with a selfie, a location and the server’s clock.

SAM BOND is an Android app for teams that work in the field. People check in from their phone. Managers see who is in, who is late and who is on leave. Admins download Excel reports. All of it lives in a Google Sheet, so there is no server to run.

Download the APK See how a day works

Needs Android 7.0 or newer. Free to install.

The day runs on five clock times

These are the default times in Dhaka. The server stamps every check-in, so changing the phone’s clock does nothing.

9:10 AMin Dhaka.

Friday is the weekly off day, and so is any holiday an admin adds: nobody is counted absent. Admins can change the night hold hours in the Admin panel. The other times are set in the code.

  1. 8:30AM

    Reminders startnow

    A phone notification asks anyone who has not marked attendance. Managers get the names of who has not, from 9:10.

  2. 9:10AM

    Late from herenow

    The office starts at 9:00 with ten minutes of grace. After that a check-in is Late Present, and the app asks for a reason.

  3. 6:00PM

    Unmarked becomes Absentnow

    Location sharing stops too, for the people an admin has ticked. Drivers carry on until they send their Day End.

  4. 9:00PM

    Night hold opensnow

    Staying away overnight? Report the place, the district and thana, and the hotel. One report per night.

  5. 6:00AM, next day

    Night hold closesnow

    A report sent at 2:00 AM counts for the night before.

What each person gets

  • Check in with a front-camera selfie and a live GPS fix. The server adds the date and time, and refuses locations from fake-GPS apps.
  • Blink, turn your head or smile when the camera asks. This live check helps show a real person is there, not a photo. An admin can switch it off.
  • Ask for Casual, Sick, Earn or Paternity leave. Attach a photo of the signed application; the phone checks that the picture looks like one.
  • See your leave balance, and your pay period (26th to 25th) on a calendar of present, late, leave, holiday and night hold days.
  • Report a night hold with your location, district, thana and hotel.
  • Get reminders and notices, order for your customers, follow your target, answer surveys and open the PDF files your admin shares.
  • Drivers add a car meter picture each morning and at the Day End. The phone reads the km number.
  • See today’s team at a glance: present, late, on leave, off, absent and pending.
  • Open a person’s selfie, and the place it was taken on the map.
  • Approve or reject leave for the people below you. Nobody can decide their own.
  • Read the night hold summary by team, for one night or a whole pay cycle.
  • Post a notice to your team, and download their attendance and night hold reports as Excel.
  • Follow a tracked person’s route on the map, when an admin has switched tracking on for them.
  • Add people, approve new account requests, set roles, choose who may use which menu, and switch people active or inactive.
  • Add holidays, post notices to everyone (with an optional SMS) and set the night hold hours.
  • Upload the target and collection Excel files. Everyone sees their own numbers; managers see their team.
  • Download Attendance and Night hold reports as Excel, or e-mail them to staff.
  • Build surveys with nine kinds of question, including picture attachments.
  • Publish a new version of the app from your phone, and make it compulsory.
  • Risky actions ask for your own password again.

Built so people can trust it

The script address is public, because anyone with the APK has it. So every rule is checked on the server, not in the app.

What it cannot do: a modified app or a rooted phone can still lie about its location. Managers looking at selfies and places is the practical check.

Read the Security page

Passwords
Stored only as salted hashes. The app keeps a signed token, encrypted on the phone, and never the password.
Guessing
Five wrong passwords pause an account for 15 minutes.
Photos
Selfies and leave pictures are visible only to the person and the managers above them.
Location
Movement tracking exists only for people an admin ticks. The phone shows a permanent notification and asks the person once.
Updates
The phone checks an update’s SHA-256 fingerprint, and Android installs it only if it carries the same signing key as the installed app.

How it fits together

  1. The Android appSelfie, location and every screen.
  2. A front doorOptional Cloudflare Worker. It adds a secret key and limits requests per address.
  3. Google Apps ScriptCode.gs checks every rule on the server.
  4. Google Sheet and DrivePeople, attendance, photos and reports. You own them.

GitHub Actions builds and signs the APK. An admin publishes it to everyone from the Admin panel, and phones update from inside the app.

Install it

  1. Download the APK

    Use the button below, or open the Releases page on GitHub.

  2. Open it and tap Install

    If Android asks, allow installs from the app you opened it from (Chrome, Files or WhatsApp). You need Android 7.0 or newer.

  3. Sign in

    Use the Employee ID and password your admin gave you. No account yet? Tap Create account. A 6-digit code goes to your e-mail, then an admin approves the request.

Download the APK

If Android says conflicting app signatures, uninstall the older copy once. Your data is safe: it lives in the Google Sheet. Want to run your own copy? The Setup guide covers the Google Sheet, the script and the GitHub build.
